# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Pilang to be a welcoming, constructive community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or platform.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions, experiments, and first contributions.
- Respect different levels of experience and communicate clearly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language, or
sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer at
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/rolandbrake/pilang/issues). Reports will be
handled privately and fairly.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation
that do not follow this code of conduct.
